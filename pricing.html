Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about payments, billing in Naira, and interview outcomes.

credit_card Can I pay in Naira with my local Nigerian bank cards or direct bank transfer?

Yes, absolutely. All prices are natively in Nigerian Naira (₦). You do NOT need a dollar-denominated card. We process payments securely through Paystack and Flutterwave, accepting standard Verve cards, Naira Mastercards, Visa cards, USSD codes, and direct bank transfers from all Nigerian banks (GTBank, Access Bank, Zenith, UBA, Kuda, etc.).

public Does ApplyNora work for both local Nigerian roles and high-paying remote USD/GBP/EUR jobs?

ApplyNora actively routes to both. You have granular control over geography filters: target top-tier local Nigerian employers, pan-African tech firms (Lagos, Nairobi, Kigali), or filter exclusively for 100% remote roles based in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and the European Union offering global compensation.

verified What is the 14-Day Interview Guarantee?

On our Executive Autopilot and Concierge tiers, if you have fully filled out your profile and do not receive at least one legitimate recruiter screening request or interview invitation within your first 14 calendar days, you may request an immediate 100% refund of your subscription fee or another month free of charge.

sync_alt Can I switch between monthly and quarterly/annual billing?

Yes, at any time through your dashboard settings. Upgrading to the Quarterly or Annual Accelerator applies an instant 25% savings calculated pro-rata against your active billing cycle.

policy Will companies or current employers know I am using an automated pipeline?

No. Our submission engine mimics human browsing patterns and uses zero-hallucination guardrails to craft highly personalized, genuine CV adaptations. Plus, you can enter your current employer's domain into the Blacklist filter to ensure no applications ever land there.
